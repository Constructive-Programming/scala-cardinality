# scala-cardinality self-measurement — 2026-11-03

The plugin measuring this repository: analyzer revision 613f655 plus the reduction passes.
First, `AnalysisWork.Exhausted` went from a budget-string × frontier-string product (which also
admitted combinations no stop can produce: a syntax-depth stop reports a source path, every other
stop an operation name) to a sum of stops: `Fuel(index|target) × operation`,
`ResolutionDepth × operation`, `SyntaxDepth × path`; failure-message text unchanged. Its
synthetic product constructor left the signature section. Second, the `Definition.Unbound` case
analysis moved into the sum (`instantiationDependent`, `opensAbstraction`), so
`Definition.instantiationDependent`/`open` dispatch through the sum instead of matching or
`isInstanceOf` on it externally — the sum owns its cases; its stored-value row is unchanged at
`5ω` (genuinely open identifier/syntax strings). Stage-3 audit verdicts for the remaining
external splits on own sums: keep as switchboards (`SingletonIntersections.resolved`, the
`Found` match in `MethodAnalysis`), companions/renderers (`FinitePart.render`), ADT
interpreters (`Shape` in the inhabitation solver), equality predicates (`Solver`'s
`Continuation.Kind`), and third-party scalameta visitors. Stage-4 pass:
`Shape.sequenceEithers` / `sequenceOptions` — already parametric, so no row can move —
swapped from a hand-rolled `foldRight` (and an `Option`-as-`Either` encoding with a throwaway
message) to cats-core's `Traverse.sequence`; leftmost-failure and order semantics preserved.
Second-run stage-2 pass: `SourceSnapshot.framedHash`'s `lengthBytes: Int` (a two-value
vocabulary `{4, 8}` that silently framed every other value as 64-bit) narrowed to the
`LengthPrefix` enum — a finite `2` row — with byte-identical hashes for both widths. The
framing dispatches from the sum (`LengthPrefix.header(length)`); a sealed-hierarchy variant
with per-case implementations measured as split singleton rows plus two extra signatures, so
the enum-with-dispatch-method form is the one kept. Published with `sbt publishLocal`, enabled
on this build via `project/plugins.sbt`. Command: `sbt cardinalityReport`. Reports are per
module (the root aggregates only). The signature section's `?` rows are the analyzer's own
resolution limits when reading its own scalameta-typed sources, not infinity claims.
